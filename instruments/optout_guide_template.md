## Your information on people-search websites: what you can do

Websites such as Spokeo, Whitepages, TruePeopleSearch, FastPeopleSearch, BeenVerified and Intelius collect information from public records and companies and show it to anyone who searches your name. You did not put it there. You can ask each site to remove it. It is free, but you must do it site by site, and information can come back later.

### Removing yourself, site by site

| Site | Where to go | What they ask for |
|---|---|---|
| TruePeopleSearch | Bottom of any page → "Remove my info" | the link to your record; a checkbox |
| FastPeopleSearch | Bottom of any page → "Remove my info" | the link to your record; an email address |
| Spokeo | spokeo.com/optout | the link to your record; an email address |
| Whitepages | whitepages.com/suppression-requests | the link to your record; a phone call with a code |
| BeenVerified | beenverified.com/app/optout/search | search, select record, email confirmation |
| Intelius | intelius.com/opt-out | search, select record, email confirmation |

Use an email address that is not your main one if you prefer. Keep a note of the date you asked. Check again after one month.

### Paid services

Companies such as DeleteMe, Incogni and Optery will send these requests for you, for a yearly fee (currently roughly 100 to 250 US dollars a year). They cover many consumer sites. They do not cover the databases used by employers, landlords, banks and government agencies.

### Your résumé on job websites

If you uploaded a résumé to a job website, it may still be there and may be visible to any employer. Log in, go to your profile or résumé settings, and set it to private or not searchable. You can also delete it.

### Your phone apps

In each app's settings you can turn off location, remove access to your contacts, and limit who can see your posts. In Facebook: Settings → Your information → Off-Facebook Activity → Clear history and Disconnect future activity.
