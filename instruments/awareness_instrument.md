# Awareness instrument (18 items)

Administered verbatim, before any lookup and before any explanation of what people-search sites or removal services are. Read each item; record the code. Do not define terms the participant does not recognise; "don't know" is a valid and informative answer.

## A. Recognition of the market (awareness index items)

| Item | Wording | Code |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Have you ever heard of companies called data brokers, companies that collect and sell information about people? | 0 no / 1 yes |
| A2 | Have you ever heard of websites where you can type a person's name and see their address, phone number or relatives? | 0 / 1 |
| A3 | Have you ever heard of services you can pay to remove your information from such websites? | 0 / 1 |
| A4 | Have you heard of any law that lets you ask companies to delete your information, for example a state deletion law? | 0 / 1 |
| A5 | If a website shows your home address, where do you think it got it? | Posted it / A company sold it / Government or public records / Don't know |
| A6 | (derived) Transaction model | 1 if A5 is "A company sold it" or "Government or public records", else 0 |

**Awareness index** = A1 + A2 + A3 + A6 (range 0 to 4).

## B. Past action

| Item | Wording | Code |
|---|---|---|
| A7 | Have you ever searched your own name on the internet? | 0 / 1 |
| A8 | Have you ever asked a company or website to delete your information? | 0 / 1 |
| A9 | Have you ever paid for a service that protects your privacy online? | 0 / 1 |
| A10 | Do you think the apps you use share your information with other companies? | 0 no / 1 yes |

## C. Predicted findability

| Item | Wording | Code |
|---|---|---|
| A11 | If someone typed your name into one of these websites, how likely is it that they would find your current home address? | 1 very unlikely / 2 unlikely / 3 likely / 4 very likely |
| A12 | How likely is it that they would find the names of your family members? | 1 to 4 |

## D. Job-site temporality

| Item | Wording | Code |
|---|---|---|
| A13 | When you apply for a job through a website like Indeed and upload your résumé, where does your résumé go? | Only that employer / Stays on the site / Not sure |
| A14 | Do you know there is a setting on such sites that controls whether other employers can see your résumé? | 0 / 1 |
| A15 | Have you ever been contacted about a job you did not apply for? | 0 / 1 |

## E. Discovery

| Item | Wording | Code |
|---|---|---|
| A16 | If you have learned that your information stays online after you give it, how did you first learn this? | Unsolicited recruiter call / Relative or friend found it / Saw it in a search / Told by an organisation / Never learned / Other |

## Administration notes

- Items A1 to A4 use the participant's language; the English terms "data broker" and "people search" are offered as glosses only after the item is answered.
- A11 and A12 are the predictions compared with the audit (RQ2). Record them before the participant sees any screen.
- Record only codes in the Awareness sheet. Observations go in the method memo.
