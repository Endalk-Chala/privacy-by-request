# Interview codes (deductive)

Applied to pseudonymised transcripts of the subset interviews. One value per participant per code unless marked "all that apply". Definitions are fixed before coding; new inductive codes are logged in the method memo and added only after the 25% double-coded sample has been compared.

| Code | Values | Definition and decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| source_attribution | Posted it / Company sold it / Government or public records / Don't know / Mixed | The participant's first full account of where the information on the people-search site came from. "Mixed" when two sources are given with equal weight. |
| imagined_searcher_records | US government / Home government / Employer / Landlord / Stalker or personal / Nobody | The first searcher named when asked who would look them up on a people-search site. Record the first; note others in notes. |
| imagined_audience_platforms | Family and community / Friends / Everyone / Employers / Government / Nobody in particular | Who the participant pictures seeing their platform posts. |
| imagined_audience_resume | Employers only / Staffing agencies / Anyone / Government / Not sure | Who the participant pictures seeing a résumé uploaded to a job site. |
| unit_of_harm | Individual / Household / Community | The level at which the participant locates the harm of exposure when discussing relatives and associates. Household when the harm is described in terms of family members' safety or standing; Community when in terms of people from the same origin group. |
| vocabulary_term | free text, transliterated | The participant's own-language term for the condition observed. Never a name or a place. |
| vocabulary_gloss | Secret / Shame / Safety / Rights / Exposed or uncovered / Other | Nearest English gloss of vocabulary_term, chosen by the coder with the participant's own explanation. |
| response_repertoire | Nothing / Ask someone / Pay / Change behaviour / Go offline / Rely on community or faith / Legal action (all that apply) | Actions the participant says they will or would take. "Pay" only when the participant says they would actually pay, not that paying exists. |
| comparison_to_home | 0 / 1 | 1 if the participant spontaneously or on probe compares the US information environment with that of their country or region of origin. Content of the comparison goes in notes without place names. |
| discovery_event_described | 0 / 1 | 1 if the participant describes a specific past moment of realising their information was online (matches A16 not equal to "Never learned"). |
| second_coder_agreement | 0 / 1 | Filled only for the 25% double-coded sample: 1 if the second coder's values matched on all codes above before discussion. |

## Reporting

Only frequencies per code (restricted tier) and approved quotations (open, in the article) are ever released. Quotations are attributed by position (for example "a recent arrival in care work"), never by region or language.
